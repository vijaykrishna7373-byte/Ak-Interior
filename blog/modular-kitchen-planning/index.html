Modular kitchen planning: begin with how you cook

A kitchen that photographs well still needs to support everyday work. Use this guide to prepare a practical starting brief before choosing a layout or finish.

Home / Modular Kitchen Planning: Layout, Storage and Materials

Describe your routine

How many people cook? What kinds of meals do you make? Do you prep in batches? These answers shape the priorities.

Map appliances and services

Note appliance sizes and existing water, gas and electrical points. Confirm what can be changed with the relevant professionals.

Think through storage

List cookware, groceries and small appliances. Keep frequent-use items easy to access.

Compare work surfaces and fronts

Ask about care, cleaning and suitability for your pattern of use, alongside appearance.

Review movement

A plan should leave comfortable room to open doors and drawers and move between work areas.

Confirm the scope

Clarify what the proposal covers, which selections are included and how installation details are agreed.
