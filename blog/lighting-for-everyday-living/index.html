Layering light for comfortable everyday living

Lighting can make a room useful at different times of day. Start by observing the daylight and tasks in each area, then plan a considered mix of sources.

Home / How to Plan Home Lighting for Everyday Living

Begin with daylight

Notice when direct sun enters, which corners remain dim and where glare can be uncomfortable.

Identify the tasks

Reading, cooking, grooming and working each need appropriate illumination in the right place.

Add comfortable general light

Ambient lighting helps you move through and use a room. Consider control and flexibility.

Use accent light sparingly

A focused beam can highlight art, texture or a feature, but should support the room rather than compete with it.

Coordinate early

Lighting positions interact with furniture, ceilings and electrical planning. Review them together during design.
