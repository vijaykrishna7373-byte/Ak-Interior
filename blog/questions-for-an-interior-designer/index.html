Questions to ask before choosing an interior designer

Choosing a design partner is easier when you understand how a project will work. Use these questions to compare proposals on the details that matter to you.

Home / Questions to Ask Before Hiring an Interior Designer

What is included in the scope?

Ask which rooms, drawings, services, products and site activities the proposal covers.

How are decisions and changes handled?

Understand how selections are approved and how changes may affect the cost or schedule.

How are materials specified?

Ask how products, finishes and alternatives are documented.

Who coordinates the work?

Clarify roles, communication channels and who to contact with questions.

What is the payment and estimate structure?

Request a clear written proposal and understand any exclusions before proceeding.

Can I see relevant completed work?

Review authentic examples and ask what parts of the project the designer delivered.
